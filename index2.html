About LearnWell AI

We believe learning should be an empowering and positive experience, not a stressful one. LearnWell AI was founded by a team of educators, engineers, and mental health advocates dedicated to creating a smarter, kinder way to learn.

Our mission is to combine the power of artificial intelligence with a deep understanding of student well-being. We're here to help every student achieve their academic goals while building healthy, sustainable study habits for life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe and secure?

Yes! This version of the app runs entirely in your browser. No data is stored on any server. We use end-to-end encryption for API calls to protect your information.

Can I use this offline?

The main interface will load, but an internet connection is required for the AI features (chat, quizzes, study plans) to work as they need to connect to the Gemini API.

Does it support multiple subjects?

Yes! Our AI supports all major subjects including Math, Science, English, History, and more. Just ask it any question you have.
